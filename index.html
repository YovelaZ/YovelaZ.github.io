Biography

Yue Zhong is currently pursuing the master's degree at the College of Computer Science and Software Engineering, Shenzhen University. She is advised by Prof. Tsz Nam Chan and Prof. Joshua Zhexue Huang.


Her research interests include spatial and spatiotemporal data mining, as well as spatial and spatiotemporal data visualization.
